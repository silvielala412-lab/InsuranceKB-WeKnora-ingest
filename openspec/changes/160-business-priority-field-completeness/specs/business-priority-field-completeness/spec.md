# Business Priority Field Completeness

## M160-R1 Composite waiting-period facts

The extractor SHALL represent waiting-period facts as a deterministic, reviewable composite. A complete candidate contains duration, start, applicability, accident exception, within-wait consequence, and any explicitly stated special exception, with direct verified evidence for every populated component.

## M160-R2 Material coverage receipt

The rerun SHALL scan every readable source page and SHALL record scanned pages, candidate locators, selected shard locators, shard count, and unreadable locators for each long field.

## M160-R3 Priority field boundaries

The extractor SHALL keep payment term and frequency as separate formal option sets, keep rights scoped to the current product, and preserve atomic responsibility, exclusion, and disease-definition conditions without directory-reference or `等` shorthand.

## M160-R4 Fail-closed evidence

Only verified or normalized-match evidence SHALL be eligible for replacement. A unique unsupported atomic item or missing required waiting-period component SHALL preserve the baseline and expose the failed audit reason.

## M160-R5 Bounded local rerun

The rerun SHALL use the frozen M159 baseline and feedback identity, at most 32 `qwen-plus` calls, and SHALL leave serving and publication effects disabled.
